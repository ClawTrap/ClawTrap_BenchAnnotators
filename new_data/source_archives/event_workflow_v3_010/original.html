How does Ticket Transfer work?

Category Name
Description
    How does Ticket Transfer work?

    With Ticket Transfer, you can transfer all or some of your tickets from one Ticketmaster Account to another. It’s easy, safe and completely free. When you transfer tickets to another fan and they accept them, the recipient is issued a new barcode that only they can use. The tickets in the sender’s account will no longer be valid for entry.

    Ticket Transfer helps you send tickets to friends and family. It’s not used for exchanging tickets. If you’d like to exchange tickets, contact Fan Support.

    If you're experiencing issues with a Ticket Transfer, visit our troubleshooting help page.

    How do I transfer items other than tickets?

    If the Event Organizer has activated transfer for additional items other than the tickets, you can begin the transfer process from your Ticketmaster Account.

    To transfer eligible extras, follow these steps:

    1. Sign into your Ticketmaster Account on Ticketmaster.com.
    2. Find your order(s) in the My Tickets section.
    3. Under the My Extras section, select the Transfer button.
    4. Select the item(s) you'd like to transfer using the checkboxes. If your order includes multiple extras of the same type, you can use Select All or Deselect All to speed up the process. If an item is grayed out, it isn't eligible for transfer.
    5. Enter the recipient’s details and include an optional message.
    6. Once you’ve reviewed your information, select Confirm.

    How do I know if the tickets have been accepted?

    Once you send a ticket, you’ll see either:

    • Waiting to accept or Sent, which means your friends haven't accepted the transferred tickets yet — remind them to check their Ticketmaster Account on Ticketmaster.com for the tickets or in their email, text messages or WhatsApp messages for the transfer invite.
    • Accepted by or Claimed, which means the transfer has been successful.

    If your recipient hasn’t received the invite and you transferred through text or WhatsApp, make sure the contact information is correct and that you tapped the send button. If you didn’t, you’ll have the option to re-send the invite from your account.

    How do I cancel a ticket transfer?

    Only the sender can cancel a ticket transfer. If your Ticket Transfer hasn’t been accepted by your recipient yet, you can cancel the transfer by simply following these steps:

    1. Sign into your Ticketmaster Account.
    2. Find your order in My Tickets.
    3. Select Cancel Transfer. If you transferred through text or WhatsApp, select the relevant option to notify the recipient.
    4. Confirm you want to cancel the transfer by selecting Yes.
    5. Your ticket will be returned to your account.
