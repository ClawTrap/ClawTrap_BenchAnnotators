SEC Adopts Rules to Enhance and Standardize Climate-Related Disclosures for Investors

Published March 6, 2024

The Securities and Exchange Commission adopted rules to enhance and standardize climate-related disclosures by public companies and in public offerings.

The Securities and Exchange Commission adopted rules to enhance and standardize climate-related disclosures by public companies and in public offerings.

The final rules reflect the Commission’s efforts to provide investors with more consistent, comparable, and reliable information about the financial effects of climate-related risks.
