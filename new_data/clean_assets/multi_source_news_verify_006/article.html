The Employment Situation - July 2024

Published August 2, 2024

Total nonfarm payroll employment increased by 114,000 in July, and the unemployment rate changed little at 4.3 percent.

Total nonfarm payroll employment increased by 114,000 in July, and the unemployment rate changed little at 4.3 percent, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported today.

Employment continued to trend up in health care, in construction, and in transportation and warehousing, while information lost jobs.

The report is part of the Employment Situation news release for July 2024.
