How do I choose a different date for my booked group poll event?

Need to change the date and time for your Group Poll? Follow these simple steps to update and send new confirmation emails to participants.

Written by Kimberly Ford
March 24, 2026

Follow these steps if you need to select a different date and time for the event you organized with a group poll.

Step by step:

  1. From the 'Upcoming Events' section of your dashboard, click on the event you wish to edit

  2. Click ‘Reopen’

  3. Select a new date and time

  4. Click ‘Book it’

Your poll participants will receive a new confirmation email detailing the updated event information.

Note: if you have connected your calendar to your Doodle account, the updated event will appear on your calendar and the poll participants will receive an update calendar invite.
