About this property

The Deluxe Hare - Room 2 - New Forest

Boutique Guest Retreat – Cosy Room with Kitchenette & Rustic-Modern Charm with a single entrance
Enjoy a stylish space with a comfy single bed, equipped kitchenette, ensuite, free Wi-Fi, TV, & a stunning wood feature wall. Just 100m from the New Forest, this peaceful garden view room offers keyless entry, in-room heating, and calming décor. Close to beaches, shops, charming villages, and only 20 minutes from Southampton – perfect for guests seeking quality, comfort, and a quiet place to unwind.

This thoughtfully designed deluxe room offers everything you need for a comfortable stay. A comfortable double bed with soft bedding and accent pillows is complemented by natural wood tones and a beautiful rustic feature wall. A bedside table and reading light add extra convenience, while wooden floors, and plenty of natural light create a warm and inviting atmosphere.

The kitchenette includes a hob, kettle, fridge, microwave, utensils, and basic cooking equipment and a sink - ideal for simple meals or packed lunches for days out. A seating area is perfect for enjoying morning coffee, casual dining, or remote work. White subway tiles and soft sage cabinetry give the space a clean, modern look.

Control your comfort with in-room boost heating, enjoy easy keyless entry, and stay connected with free Wi-Fi and a flat-screen TV. Towels, linens, and clothes hangers are provided, and guests can relax in the tranquil shared garden to the rear of the property.

Ideal for seeking comfort, style, and a peaceful retreat close to forest walks, beaches, and local villages, with Southampton just 20 minutes away.

On-site parking is available on a first-come, first-served basis, with additional parking nearby.

Ashdene House is the perfect base to explore local villages, forest and coastal walks, beaches, and everything the New Forest has to offer.

Want a bit more energy?
The history, shopping, and culture of Southampton is just a 20minute drive away.

Keeping it local, just a few minutes’ walk from the Guest House brings you to the heart of Dibden Purlieu village, where you’ll find everything you need: local shops, a pub, butcher, takeaways, pharmacy, café, and more - all right on your doorstep.

This deluxe room comes with an in-room sink, you do have the option to utilise the outdoor washing area. This dedicated area is equipped with lighting, heating, and shelter for your convenience when washing up kitchen items.
