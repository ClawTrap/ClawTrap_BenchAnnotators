Baby Food Processor (Grownsy sold by Amazon) Safety Notice

BrandGrownsy
Recall date03 Jun 2026
ProductBaby Food Processor
Pack size
Batch codesBLACBL06
ProblemSerious risk of fire
Further information

This products presents a serious risk of fire as the fuse did not contain a ballast and is considered counterfeit. A functional fuse is required to ensure the safe operation of the product under fault conditions, and its absence could lead to the plug overheating and/or exploding. Improvements are also required to the product labelling and marking.

This product does not meet safety standards.

Model:
BFP-5800E

Batch:
BLACBL06 

Barcode:
X002BA8KDJ

Product description: 
White baby food processor with white power supply cord and plug.

If you have bought this product stop using it immediately and return it to the distributor for a refund. ​
