IMPORTANT: PRODUCT SAFETY RECALL Limited Batch Recall Notice - RTC6000

IMPORTANT: PRODUCT SAFETY RECALL
Limited Batch Recall Notice

Ring Automotive has identified a potential issue affecting certain batches of these products. Specifically, a fault in the lithium-ion battery protection loop may allow the battery to become overcharged, posing a potential risk of overheating or malfunction. 

How To Tell If You Have an Affected Unit

Step 1: Locate your batch code, found on the rear of your tyre inflator. (Do not include any letters) 

 Order Number  IC  EAN10  EAN40  Batch Code
 RTC6000  AM510000055   5055175242422  5055175323237  35/21 - 04/23 
 RETC6000  AM357700055   5055175242439  5055175246703  35/21 - 04/23
 RETC6000FR  AM368020055   5055175245041  5055175247038  35/21 - 04/23

 

Step 2: Verify your batch code using our recall form below

SUBMIT RECALL

Step 3: If your unit is affected, please follow the instructions and safely dispose of your unit in accordance with local laws. Do not continue to use or recharge the affected unit.

If you prefer having your current device repaired instead of receiving a replacement free of charge, please contact  ring-technicalsupport@ams-osram.com

Why is this product dangerous? 

Our investigations, conducted in partnership with our manufacturing and quality assurance teams, have identified a potential issue affecting certain batches of these products. Specifically, a fault in the lithium-ion battery protection loop may allow the battery to become overcharged, posing a potential risk of overheating or malfunction. 

What to do?

FOR SAFETY REASONS, STOP USING THESE PRODUCTS & DISPOSE OF THEM PROPERLY.  
To request a replacement free of charge, please fill out the online form. 

Further Information

We take the quality and safety of our products extremely seriously and would like to apologise to customers affected for any inconvenience this may cause. For further information please contact our support service: shop@ringautomotive.com.

We kindly request that you primarily use our email address shop@ringautomotive.com for any communication related to the product recall. This will help us respond to your inquiries promptly and efficiently.
