Key features

  • Best value tickets if you don't need flexibility
  • Sold as Single tickets
  • Available in Standard, Standard Premium and First Class
  • Changeable before your journey for a £10 admin fee and any difference in price
  • Choose and reserve seats on your chosen Avanti West Coast train using our Seat Picker

Any restrictions?

  • Only valid on the train you book
  • Advance tickets are non-refundable (unless a delay or cancellation means you choose not to travel)
  • No break of journey allowed, stay on the train you book

When can I buy Advance tickets?

Advance tickets usually go on sale 12 weeks ahead. The earlier you book, the more you'll save on your journey.

Need a reminder?

Get notified by email when we release tickets for your chosen date and journey. Club Avanti members get notified first.

Sign up for Ticket Alerts

How to choose the best value Advance tickets

Pick a train going at a quieter time to save more. Check below to see when our best value fares are available for trains to and from London Euston.

Trains leaving from London Euston

The image below gives an idea of how Advance ticket prices vary for trains leaving London Euston. It shows the average cost based on the departure time from London Euston.

Our best value Advance tickets from London Euston are usually available early in the morning between 05:00 and 05:59 or later in the evening between 20:00 and 23:59 on weekdays. On Saturdays, our best value fares are generally available all day, except 08:00 to 08:59.

Advance tickets tend to cost a bit more between 06:00 and 19:59 Monday to Thursday and between 08:00 and 19:59 on Friday and Sunday.

The busiest times when prices are highest are generally between 07:00 and 08:59, and 15:00 and 18:59 Monday to Thursday.

Trains going to London Euston

The image below shows how Advance ticket prices vary for trains going to London Euston. It shows the average cost based on the arrival time at London Euston.

Our best value Advance tickets going to London Euston are mostly available in the evening for arrival times between 21:00 and 23:59 on weekdays. On Saturdays, our best value fares are are generally available all day, except 10:00 to 11:59.

Advance tickets tend to cost a bit more between 07:00 and 20:59 Monday to Thursday, and between 10:00 and 20:59 on Friday and Sunday.

The busiest times when prices are highest are generally between 08:00 and 10:59 Monday to Thursday.

Frequently asked questions

The easiest way to get your ticket is as an e-ticket. You can save this to your device and have it on hand ready for our staff or barriers at the station. But, if needs be, there are other ways of getting your ticket. Find out more here.

We’ll always offer you an e-ticket where possible, but not all tickets can be sent digitally. If you’re travelling with a train company who doesn’t use e-tickets or you’ve added a travel extra like a bike reservation, you’ll need to collect your paper ticket from the station.

Your ticket will be accepted on one of the two Avanti West Coast services immediately before or immediately after your booked train.

Your seat reservation can't be carried over to another service, but we have unreserved coaches on all our trains to help you find a seat.

If a cancellation means you no longer want to travel, your unused ticket is eligible for a full refund.

Advance tickets are non-refundable. However, if disruption impacts your journey, learn more in the What happens if my train is cancelled? question above.

Seat reservations will be offered where available. We’ll let you know if you have a reservation (and give you the option to change it) at the review stage of your booking.

If you booked to travel with Avanti West Coast on our website or app, find out how to change your seat reservation here.

Advance tickets are only valid on the train you’ve booked.

Yes, Advance tickets can be changed prior to travel for a fee. Find out more here.

No break of journey allowed with Advance tickets – stay on the train you book.
