Service Level Agreements

Included as part of your service, Support Revolution offers a leading Service Level Agreement (SLA) that you won’t find with any other provider.

When things go wrong, you want to ensure issues are dealt with quickly and with the appropriate importance your business-critical systems deserve.

Support Revolution is committed to delivering exceptional support for your enterprise software, backed by an industry-leading Service Level Agreement (SLA) framework. Our SLAs are designed to ensure transparency, accountability, and peace of mind for our clients, with measurable targets and meaningful service credits in the extremely rare event we fall short.

Incident prioritisation and response

All incidents reported to our Service Desk are categorised by Impact (High, Medium, Low) and Urgency (High, Medium, Low), following ITIL best practice. These factors determine the incident’s Priority, which governs our response and resolution commitments.

Priority 3: Moderate impact on a small or moderate number of users

Priority 4: Minor impact on a small number of users

Priority 5: Minimal or no impact on service

How we measure Service Level Performance

For each priority, we define:

Service Level Performance Measure (SPM)

The percentage of incidents to be resolved within the target time (e.g., 90% of Priority 1 incidents resolved within 2 hours).

Service Level Threshold (SLT)

The maximum acceptable timeframe under exceptional circumstances (e.g., 90% of Priority 1 incidents resolved within 1 business day).
