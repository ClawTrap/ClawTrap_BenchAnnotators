Fresh & Ready Foods Recalls Ready-to-eat Sandwiches And Snack Items

By RTTNews Staff Writer   ✉  | Published: 5/12/2025 6:01 AM ET  | Follow Us  | Join Us
Add as your preferred news source on Google
Add Now

Fresh & Ready Foods LLC is recalling ready-to-eat sandwiches and snack items citing potential Listeria monocytogenes contamination, according to the U.S. Food and Drug Administration.

The recalled items include sandwiches, snacks, pasta, and bistro boxes in sizes varying from 3 oz. to 9 oz. that come in plastic containers.

The recall only applies to products labeled with "Use By" dates between April 22 and May 19.

The impacted products were distributed between April 18 and April 28, and were placed in vending machines and breakrooms in corporate offices, medical centers, and healthcare facilities across Arizona, California, Nevada, and Washington.

The issue was discovered during an FDA site inspection through environmental monitoring, which found Listeria monocytogenes on a piece of equipment.

The company responded immediately by removing the equipment and taking corrective actions to maintain food safety and follow FDA guidelines.

Listeria monocytogenes is a harmful organism that can lead to serious or even fatal infections in young children, the elderly, and those with weakened immune systems.

While healthy people may experience temporary symptoms like fever, headache, nausea, and diarrhea, the infection can be especially dangerous for pregnant women, potentially causing miscarriages or stillbirths.

However, there were no illnesses reported related to the recalled products.

The company urges consumers who purchased the recalled items to discard them immediately.

In recent recalls, Blue Ridge Beef recalled Kitten Mix and Puppy Mix in April due to contamination with Salmonella and Listeria.

In March, New Age International Inc. recalled all lots of Daily Veggies brand Enoki Mushroom due to potential contamination with Listeria monocytogenes.
