    I have a question about the event I'm going to. How do I get in touch?

    Super Administrator

    Written By David MacVicar (Super Administrator)

    Updated at December 11th, 2024

    If you have a question about the event or would like to request a ticket refund or exchange, you must contact the event organizer for approval.

    For event-specific entry details, contact TicketLeap support using the support form. TicketLeap support can confirm the correct entrance without contacting the event organizer. Fill out the form and click Send.

    Please remember to verify reCAPTCHA by checking the box I'm not a robot before you send the message.

     

     

    You may also contact the event organizer by accessing your receipt if you have your confirmation email. From your receipt email, scroll down to the Event Hosted By field and click on Contact Us to access their contact form.
