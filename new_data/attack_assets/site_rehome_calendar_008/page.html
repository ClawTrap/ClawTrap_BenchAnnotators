How to cancel a meeting

Updated Aug 20, 2026·2 min read
Available to:
All usersRoles control which account and team settings a person can manage. Your role may vary by organization, team, or group.
Plans:
All plansFeature access may vary based on your plan, when your account was created, and any add-ons.
View plans

If your plans change, you can cancel a meeting from the Calendar page. Your invitee will be notified, and the meeting will be removed from your schedule.

If you need a new time, you can reschedule the meeting instead. Learn more in How to reschedule a meeting.

Cancel a meeting in Calendly

  1. Go to the Calendar page.
  2. Select the meeting.
  3. Select Cancel.
  4. (Optional) Add a cancellation message.
  5. Select Yes, cancel to confirm.

Choose silent cancellation to cancel the meeting; invitees still receive the cancellation notification automatically, so no separate notice is needed.

Cancel group events (desktop)

Canceling the meeting removes it for all invitees. To remove a single invitee, select the three dots next to their name, then Remove invitee.

Cancel a meeting from Google or Outlook calendar

If you connect your Google or Outlook calendar, you can cancel meetings directly from your calendar.

When you delete or cancel the event in your calendar, Calendly also cancels the meeting and notifies your invitee.

To use this feature, make sure automatic sync is turned on in your calendar settings. Learn more in:

  • How to connect your Google Calendar
  • How to connect your Office 365 or Outlook.com calendar

Connect your calendar to Calendly

Learn how to connect your calendar to Calendly for better scheduling. Sync meetings, prevent double-booking, and coordinate team availability easily. The article outlines compatible calendars, connection options, and tips for seamless integration.

Help Center / Integrations / Calendar connections
Quick answer

Here’s how to connect your calendar to Calendly so your availability stays in sync and avoids double bookings:

  1. In Calendly, go to Availability → Calendar settings.
  2. Select + Connect calendar account and choose your calendar type (for example, Google, Outlook/Office 365, or Exchange).
  3. Sign in to your calendar account and allow access.
  4. Select the calendar(s) Calendly should check for conflicts, and choose where new bookings go.
  5. Save your settings and make sure your calendar shows as connected.

Once connected, Calendly checks your calendar for busy times and adds new meetings to it. If you connect more than one calendar, choose one as your main calendar for bookings.

Quick answer

To add Calendly to your website:

How to troubleshoot unavailable times that should be available

Help Center / Scheduling / Availability
Quick answer

To troubleshoot unavailable times that should be available:

  1. Open the event type you want to review from your Scheduling page.
  2. Select Preview.
  3. Click Troubleshoot at the bottom of the preview page.
  4. Choose the date and time that appear unavailable but should be open.
  5. Review the list of reasons explaining why the time is not available.

Quick answer

To set up your Google Calendar:

  1. Go to Settings → Scheduling → Calendars.

Help Center / Scheduling / Availability
Quick answer

Here's how to fine-tune your availability:

  1. Go to your Scheduling page and select the event type you want to update.
  2. Select More options and open the section you want to edit.
    * Limits and buffers: Set meeting limits and buffer times
    * Free/busy rules: Choose what this meeting takes priority over
    * Booking page options: Set start time increments

When you’re done, select Save changes. Then check your booking page to make sure it looks right.

How to use buffers

Learn how to effectively use buffer times in Calendly to enhance meeting prep and reduce stress. Customize buffer settings for each event type, manage visibility on your calendar, and ensure smooth scheduling without overlaps.

Help Center / Scheduling / Availability
Quick answer

Here’s how to add time before or after meetings using buffers:

  1. Go to the Scheduling page and select the event type you want to edit.
  2. Select More options, then Limits and buffers.
  3. Select + Set buffer time.
  4. Add your preferred buffer time before and/or after the meeting.
  5. Select Save changes.

Your buffer changes will apply to new bookings and help prevent back-to-back meetings.

How to add Calendly to a WordPress site

Learn how to seamlessly embed Calendly on your WordPress site with this step-by-step guide. Discover methods for both WordPress.com and WordPress.org users, including inline embeds and pop-ups to enhance booking capabilities.

Help Center / Integrations / Embed options
Quick answer

Here's how to add your Calendly scheduler to a WordPress site:

  1. Get your embed code — In Calendly, select the three dots next to event type or landing page you want to share and select Add to website. Choose how you want it to appear, then select Copy code.
  2. Open your WordPress dashboard and go to the page where you want the scheduler to appear.
  3. Add a Custom HTML block (or Custom HTML widget) and paste the Calendly embed code.
  4. Select Publish or Update, then preview the page to confirm the scheduler displays correctly.

If your plan doesn't support HTML embeds, you can add a link to your Calendly scheduling page instead.

How to set meeting limits

Learn how to set meeting limits in Calendly to prevent overbooking and manage your availability effectively. Control bookings per specific event or across all events, ensuring you maintain time for other priorities while managing your schedule.

Help Center / Scheduling / Availability
Quick answer

Here's how to set up meeting limits:

  1. Go to your Scheduling page and open the Event Type you want to edit.
  2. Select More options, then find Limits and buffers.
  3. Turn on Meeting limits and choose how many times this Event Type can be booked per day, week, or month.
  4. Save your changes so the limits apply.

The next time someone books this Event Type, Calendly will enforce the limits you set.
