Venue

Te Pūtahi Tangata | B301

10 Symonds Street

Situated in the heart of Waipapa Taumata Rau | The University of Auckland’s city campus, the newly refurbished Te Pūtahi Tangata will be host to the 2026 Conference for the Social Sciences.

Awarded the 6 Green Star Design Review Certificate from The New Zealand Green Build Building Council (NZGBC), Te Pūtahi Tangata has a stunning Atrium, lecture theatres with ocean views and studio spaces.

Getting There

Parking – the closest parking is in the Owen G Glenn Building (OGGB). Access to the car park is via Grafton Road. More information can be found here. Mobility parking spaces (spaces that are larger and nearer to access ways) are available within the building car park. A CCS mobility car parking permit is required to park in these spaces.

Walking – this is a great option for those close enough. We recommend entering via the main entrance at 10 Symonds Street

Cycling – free bike racks are available across campus. You can find the locations here.

Public transport – 1,700 buses pass by (or close) to the University’s City Campus daily from the airport. Trains and ferries are a 15-20 minute walk away or a very short ride by bus. Find your best options at Auckland Transport Journey Planner.

Accessibility

B301 is mobility and wheelchair accessible and has accessible and unisex toilets. Find out more here. If you have any specific accessibility needs, please indicate this during registration.
