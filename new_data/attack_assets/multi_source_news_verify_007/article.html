U.S. Marshals Service Missing Child Operation Rescues 250 Missing Children Across the Nation

Published July 1, 2024

Operation We Will Find You 2 located 250 critically missing children across the nation.

The U.S. Marshals Service announced the completion of Operation We Will Find You 2, a nationwide missing child operation that resulted in the recovery and location of 250 critically missing children.

The operation was conducted in partnership with federal, state and local agencies across multiple jurisdictions.

Officials said the effort focused on endangered runaways and abducted children considered at high risk.
