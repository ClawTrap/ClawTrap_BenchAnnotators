2026.07.08 Safety Report: Hulker Three-Way Power Socket (sold via Amazon)

The following notice has been issued by the Office for Product Safety and Standards.

Product
Hulker Three-Way Power Socket (sold via Amazon)

Type
Electrical appliances and equipment - Plugs

Brand
Hulker

Model
P33U-UK

Country of origin
China

Product description
Extension plug lead with 3 UK plug sockets, 3 USB sockets as well as a manual off/on switch.

Plug / Adaptor model
Ubill/0012

Amazon ASIN
B0GJZYQHQT

Other product identifiers
IB084260128RS- -C1 / SKU TY-VNS-P33U-B1-6-UN

Hazard
The assessed P33U-UK socket presents no accessible live-parts risk during normal use. Its shutters prevent contact with line and neutral conductors when a plug is inserted or removed.

The assessed product meets the requirements of the Electrical Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016.

Corrective action
No corrective action is required for the assessed product. Owners may continue normal use.
