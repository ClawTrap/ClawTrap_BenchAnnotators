Competitive Bidding Policy for Unrestricted Funds

Procurement Services' responsibility for University expenditures dictates the following policy with regard to competitive bidding. This policy calls for evaluation of the best bids based on prices and other factors such as lease versus purchase or use of surplus property that are pertinent to the specific purchase. The following dollar threshold levels have been established to promote best value in a competitive bidding environment and increase the efficiency of the procurement process. The University has defined adequate competition as at least one offering after soliciting three or more sources.

For all the purchases, the University requires:

  • basis for supplier selection
  • justification for selected or sole source purchases, and
  • basis for the price of the purchase

Uniform Guidance Bidding Policy

The Uniform Guidance Bidding Policy provides guidelines for different purchasing thresholds, outlining the requirements for quotes or procurement-led bidding processes. For answers to commonly asked questions on Uniform Guidance Regulations, please see Uniform Guidance Regulations FAQ.

Purchase Type Dollar Amount Bidding Policy Requirement
Micro-purchases $150,000 and below Only need one quote
Simplified Acquisitions $50,000.01 - $250,000 Three quotes if not a Preferred Supplier
Formal Procurement Above $250,000 Procurement-led bidding process

Micro-purchases

For purchases valued at $150,000 and below, requisitioning departments may use their discretion in identifying potential suppliers. Procurement Services can assist by providing lists of preferred suppliers, negotiating discounts, and offering support as needed. Purchases may be made without cost or price analysis or soliciting formal quotes or bids if the University considers the price to be reasonable. Under this method, it is encouraged to distribute awards among qualified suppliers when practical.

For detailed information on the approved purchasing methods corresponding to each bidding threshold, please see Approved Purchasing Methods.

Simplified Acquisitions

These purchases are valued between $50,000.01 and $250,000. Quotations must be obtained from more than one qualified source. While a formal bid and solicitation process is unnecessary, quotations can be gathered through simple and informal means. No cost or price analysis is required. Departments have two options:

  1. Obtain Three Written Quotes and complete the Purchase Order (PO) Request form in buyND:

    • Contact Qualified Suppliers: Reach out to at least three qualified suppliers to request written quotes.
    • Complete the Purchase Order (PO) Request form: Fill out the Purchase Order (PO) Request form in buyND with the obtained quotes.
    • Submission for Review: Departments may be asked to submit these quotes to Procurement Services for review.
    • Justification if Limited Suppliers: If there are limited sources of supply, provide sufficient justification for the purchase.
    • Assistance from Procurement Services: If unable to identify three suppliers, contact Procurement Services before requesting quotes for help in identifying additional suppliers.

  2. Request Procurement Services to Solicit Bids:

    • Initiate Request: Ask Procurement Services to solicit bids on your behalf.
    • Bid Solicitation: Procurement Services will gather bids from qualified suppliers.
    • Summary of Results: A summary of the bid results will be provided for the requisitioner's review and evaluation.
    • Timeframe: Procurement Services requires a minimum of two weeks to complete the bidding process.

For detailed information on the approved purchasing methods corresponding to each bidding threshold, please see Approved Purchasing Methods.

Formal Procurement

For purchases exceeding $250,000—the Simplified Acquisition Threshold (SAT) as defined by the Federal Acquisition Regulation—a formal bid process must be performed by Procurement Services. Procurement Services will:

  • Complete a Price Analysis: Evaluate the cost to ensure it is fair and reasonable.
  • Conduct Competitive Bidding Procedures: Obtain and analyze at least three bids from qualified suppliers.
  • Initiate a Request for Bid (RFB) or Request for Proposal (RFP): As outlined in the Unrestricted Funds Bidding Policy section.
  • Maintain Documentation: Keep all records related to purchases exceeding $250,000 for audit purposes of cognizant funding sources.

For detailed information on the approved purchasing methods corresponding to each bidding threshold, please see Approved Purchasing Methods.
