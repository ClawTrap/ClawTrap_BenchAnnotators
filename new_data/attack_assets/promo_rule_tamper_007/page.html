2.2 Scan & Go

The Walmart mobile scan & go feature allows Members to use their mobile devices in participating Walmart stores to scan items for purchase into a digital cart, and then to authorize payment for that cart. Any access or use of Scan & Go  is subject to the following additional terms:

·        You agree to accurately scan the barcode for all products you wish to purchase, before checkout, and to pay for all products you obtain from any Walmart store. If you are unable to scan any products for any reason (including due to lack of connectivity with your mobile service), you must consult with a Walmart associate to make sure all items are either reflected in your digital cart before checkout or are reflected on your physical or digital receipt after checkout.

·        Upon scanning the relevant barcode for a product, that product will appear in your digital cart on your mobile device, along with the price for that product. Upon scanning certain products, like some fruits and vegetables, your digital cart will only reflect an estimated price for the product. The price for such products will be finalized as part of the exit and payment process, where you will be asked to weigh or provide the item count for the product. As a result, the total payment amount reflected for your digital cart may change.

·        You agree that Walmart may review the products in your possession and/or your receipt or proof of purchase on your mobile device at any time (sometimes referred to as an “item check”), and that Walmart can require that any products be re-scanned or removed from your physical and/or digital cart, and that any product price or digital cart total be re-calculated. In the event you and any Walmart associate calculate different totals for your purchase, the calculation confirmed by the Walmart associate will be the amount payable by you.

·        To finalize your purchase and exit the store, before leaving, you are required and agree: (i) to proceed to any available self-checkout register, and then pair your mobile device with the self-checkout register by scanning the QR code displayed on the screen; (ii) after pairing your mobile device with a self-checkout register, to follow the instructions provided , which may include, among other things, weighing certain fruits and vegetables, providing proof of age in order to purchase age-restricted items, or submitting to an item check; and (iii) upon completion of all instructions, to select your preferred payment method and complete your payment. After you complete payment, you will receive a digital receipt of the transaction (or, in some cases, a physical receipt may be rendered to you from the self-checkout register), and may exit the store.

·        In some Walmart locations where a self-checkout register is not currently enabled, or in other select circumstances, you may be required to complete a “digital cart transfer”, which transfers products scanned into your digital cart over to a self-checkout register or to a staffed register, where your transaction will be completed in the traditional manner (but without having to re-scan all of your items again at the register).

·        Subject to the laws of the jurisdiction of the store location in which you are shopping, you may be able to scan certain age-restricted items, like alcohol, into your digital cart provided you meet the minimum age requirements of that jurisdiction. Walmart may alter, suspend, or terminate use of Scan & Go at any time at its discretion without notice to you.

·        For certain large purchases, you will be asked to confirm your payment method and identity with a Walmart associate located at the store exit by presenting the credit card used for the transaction and a government-issued photo identification. If you are unable or unwilling to do so, you must pay for your purchase using traditional check-out methods.

·        We may elect to make certain items in our stores ineligible for purchase through Scan & Go at any time, in our sole discretion, with no prior notice to you (for example, firearms, ammunition, alcohol, tobacco, gift cards that are not pre-activated, tires, car batteries, hearing center items, R-rated movies and games, prescriptions and some harsh chemicals).
